Each card below is one person in that folder — "Person #N", numbered fresh within each folder rather than by a global id that only ever grows, so "Person #2 in this week's folder" stays meaningful even after reclustering. A person the app hasn't confidently grouped with anyone else yet still gets a Person # of their own, marked "not yet matched" — they're just as much a person as any other card, only with one photo so far. Tick "Select to group" on two or more not-yet-matched cards (even across different folders — the same person often only shows up once per week) and use the bar above to merge them into a single person in one go. Click any thumbnail to see every photo for that person, and use the move control there to fix a wrong grouping: pull a face out of a cluster it doesn't belong in, or drop it into the cluster it should have joined.
